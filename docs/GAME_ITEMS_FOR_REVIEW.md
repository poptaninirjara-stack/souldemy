## Hedge-o-meter (`r1-hedge`)

Source: Lesson 5 and Exercise 5a model answers; the weaker options are new.

- Loads of people think the exam system is unfair. → **Many critics argue that the examination system is unfair.** (other options: Everyone knows the exam system is totally unfair. / The exam system is obviously unfair to loads of people.)
- Crime goes up when there are no jobs. → **Unemployment is often associated with higher crime rates.** (other options: Unemployment always causes crime. / Crime definitely rises when people have no jobs.)
- Poetry is the best way to show feelings. → **Poetry is often regarded as an effective means of expressing emotion.** (other options: Poetry proves that feelings are best shown in verse. / Poetry is the best and only way to show feelings.)
- Sadly, a lot of folk are getting poorer. → **Poverty appears to be increasing among a significant proportion of the population.** (other options: Sadly, many people are clearly getting poorer. / Loads more folk are poor now, which is shocking.)
- Education reduces crime. → **Education tends to be associated with lower crime rates.** (other options: Education always reduces crime. / Education proves that crime will fall.)
- This study proves that social media causes anxiety. → **This study suggests that heavy social media use may be linked to anxiety.** (other options: This study clearly proves that social media causes anxiety in everyone. / Social media obviously causes anxiety, as this study shows.)
